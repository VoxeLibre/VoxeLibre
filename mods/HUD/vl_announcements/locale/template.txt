The Sneaky Release=

Tridents have finally arrived, together with new enchantments, richer ambient audio, and many improvements throughout the game.=

Tridents=

Find this powerful spear as rare structure loot and use it underwater or in the air.=

New enchantments=
Swift Sneak and Impaling open up new movement and combat options.=
Ambient audio=
Water, lava, caves, fire, and sculk received new or improved sounds.=
More equipment=
Leather horse armor and the decorative star are now available.=
Changes=

Mob behavior, spawning, vision, activation, and drops received numerous improvements.=

The HUD bars and farming systems were reworked.=
Touchscreen shield controls and many other mobile interactions were improved.=
Fixes=

Fixed issues with crafting results, buckets, item frames, portals, and water bottles.=

Fixed crashes involving combat, campfires, mobs, and projectiles.=
The Dynamic Release=

This release introduced live game-rule tuning, new equipment and decorations, and broad combat and gameplay rebalancing.=

Dynamic settings=
Administrators can tune supported settings and game rules while playing.=
New equipment=
Discover deepslate tools, craftable chainmail armor, and golden decorations.=
Combat balancing=
Potions, stews, enchantments, mobs, and combat received extensive balancing.=

The creative inventory was reorganized and fire spreading became more predictable.=

Fire Elementals replaced Blazes as the first member of a broader elemental mob family.=

Some gameplay bugs and compatibility issues were fixed.=
The On Display Release=

Maps, signs, item frames, world generation, and mob spawning all received major upgrades.=

Better maps=
Maps can be zoomed out with the cartography table and look better while held.=
Display improvements=
Signs and item frames gained new placement and display capabilities.=
Sit and relax=
Players can sit on supported blocks, or sit and lie down using commands.=
Structure generation became more deterministic.=

Mob spawning, performance, textures, sounds, and model compatibility were improved.=

Item duplication, world generation, and other bugs were fixed.=
