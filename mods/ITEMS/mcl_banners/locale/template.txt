Banner=
Banners are tall colorful decorative blocks. They can be placed on the floor and at walls. Banners can be emblazoned with a variety of patterns using a loom.=
Use a loom with a banner and dye to add a pattern. Some patterns also require a banner pattern item. Emblazoned banners can be emblazoned again to combine various patterns.=
You can copy the pattern of a banner by placing two banners of the same color in the crafting grid—one needs to be emblazoned, the other one must be clean. Finally, you can use a banner on a cauldron with water to wash off its top-most layer.=
